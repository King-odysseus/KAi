# KAI 1.2.41

## Fixes

- The Guided Build card can be folded. A new chevron beside the Plan and team button folds the card to one slim bar (step, progress, Plan and team) so the conversation gets the room, and unfolds it again. The choice is remembered. The cross still hides the card completely, and its tooltip now says where it comes back from.
- The card is at most half the window high (it was 62%) and scrolls inside itself.
- Plan rows were hard to read: every step except the current one picked up the default grey button background. Each row now sets its own colours, with a clear outline on the current step and a coloured tick on finished steps.

## Version and use

- Desktop version: 1.2.41 (patch release; no breaking changes).
- Restart KAI after installing the updated desktop build.
- Roll back by installing the prior 1.2.40 build.
